OUR PURPOSE

Public services should be easier to understand.

SevaNavigator aims to make public-service information easier to navigate by explaining processes, highlighting documents to check, and directing people towards appropriate official sources.

Clarity first

Explain the process in straightforward language so people know what to check before applying.

Official sources

Help users find the responsible authority rather than pretending to process applications ourselves.

Privacy by design

Never ask users to submit identity documents, passwords, OTPs, or banking information here.

IMPORTANT INFORMATION

Know what this website does—and doesn't do.

SevaNavigator is not a government website.

It is an independent informational guide. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or operated by any Indian government department unless a specific relationship is explicitly established.

  • Verify requirements: official rules, eligibility, fees, and document requirements can change.
  • Check website addresses: follow links carefully and confirm that you are visiting the intended official service.
  • Protect your information: never provide passwords, OTPs, or banking details to this guide.
  • No application processing: SevaNavigator does not issue certificates, approve applications, collect government fees, or guarantee a service outcome.
