SevaNavigator

One place for government services. Less searching, less confusion, more time saved.

🌐 Live Website: https://sevanavigator.netlify.app

About

SevaNavigator is a centralized discovery platform for Indian government services, built to make accessing public-service information simpler, faster, and less confusing.

People often have to visit multiple websites to understand a single application. Important details can be difficult to find, instructions may be unclear, and users may struggle to identify the correct official portal. SevaNavigator aims to reduce this hassle by bringing useful service information, application guidance, checklists, and official links together in one accessible place.

Instead of spending time navigating scattered websites, users can explore services, understand the application process, review requirements, and find the relevant official portal from one platform.

What SevaNavigator Helps With

- Save time: Find information about multiple government services without repeatedly searching across different websites.
- Explore services in one place: Search and filter available services to find what you need.
- Understand application requirements: Review eligibility criteria, application steps, fees, and document checklists where information is available.
- Stay organized: Use helpful checklists and timer-related tools where available to keep track of application-related tasks.
- Understand possible rejection reasons: Learn about common reasons applications may be rejected, where relevant information is provided.
- Reach official portals: Follow links to the relevant government websites to access official services and complete applications.
- Navigate with greater confidence: Find essential information in a more organized, user-friendly format.

Suggestions and Feedback

SevaNavigator is designed to improve with the needs of its users. Users can submit suggestions directly through the website to recommend new government services, request useful features, report issues, or share ideas that could make the platform more helpful and easier to use.

User feedback helps identify missing information, improve the user experience, and guide future updates to the platform.

Our Goal

The goal of SevaNavigator is to reduce the time and confusion involved in finding and understanding Indian government services. It is designed to make the process more convenient by organizing useful information in one place—not by replacing official government portals.
